About ADN Semiconductors

Engineering the Silicon Behind Ambitious Products

ADN Semiconductors is a semiconductor engineering partner helping product teams turn complex hardware requirements into reliable, production-ready silicon.

From RTL design and verification to FPGA prototyping, custom IP, system-level validation, synthesis, and timing analysis, we bring disciplined engineering to the front end of the semiconductor development lifecycle.

Built for Complex Programs

Semiconductor development leaves little room for avoidable risk. Our engineers work alongside product and design teams to bring structure, technical depth, and execution discipline to every stage of a program.

We support ASIC, SoC, and FPGA initiatives with engineering practices focused on:

  • Design quality - clean, synthesizable, maintainable RTL
  • Verification confidence - structured verification, coverage, assertions, and closure
  • System readiness - hardware/software integration, prototyping, and bring-up support
  • Execution discipline - clear milestones, documentation, risk tracking, and transparent communication
  • Technical depth - experienced engineers working with modern semiconductor methodologies and tools

Engineering From Bangladesh. Built for the Global Semiconductor Industry.

ADN Semiconductors is part of ADN Group, bringing the stability of an established technology organization together with a focused semiconductor engineering team.

Our ambition is bigger than delivering individual projects. We are building a sustainable semiconductor engineering capability in Bangladesh - developing people, strengthening technical expertise, and creating a globally competitive engineering organization.

Engineering + Talent Development

We believe strong semiconductor companies are built by strong engineers.

Through ADN Semiconductors Academy, we complement our engineering practice with hands-on training in RTL design, SystemVerilog, verification, UVM, and related semiconductor workflows. Our training is shaped by practical engineering experience, helping build a stronger pipeline of semiconductor talent for Bangladesh and the global industry.

Our Commitment

We aim to be the team clients trust when the design is complex, the schedule matters, and engineering quality cannot be compromised.

Let's build better silicon, together.

Delivery Model

How We Execute

  1. 01

    Align

    Goals and fit

    Discuss high-level goals, context, and constraints to confirm a good fit.

  2. 02

    Protect

    Mutual NDA

    Sign a mutual NDA before sharing sensitive technical or business details.

  3. 03

    Define

    Requirements and quote

    Define requirements, deliverables, milestones, and price quotes.

  4. 04

    Kickoff

    Contract and communication

    Sign the contract, set communication channels, and agree on meeting cadences.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Milestones and feedback

    Share progress at agreed milestones and incorporate feedback throughout execution.

  6. 06

    Close

    Acceptance and handoff

    Deliver final code, test benches, and documentation, then complete acceptance testing and project closure.

Technical Depth

Tech Stack

RTL and Digital Design

SystemVerilog and Verilog based implementation for high‑performance and low‑power digital subsystems, using Synopsys and Cadence design flows.

Verification

UVM‑based environments, constrained‑random testing, assertions, coverage strategy, and debug closure across Synopsys and Cadence verification flows.

DFT and Test Readiness

Scan architecture support, test planning coordination, and integration practices aligned with production goals.

FPGA and Prototyping

Rapid prototyping flows using AMD Xilinx platforms to de‑risk integration and accelerate hardware‑software co‑validation.

Embedded and Bring-Up

Firmware integration and platform-level debug support for smoother lab and pre-production transitions.

EDA Automation

Scripted workflows, regression orchestration, and productivity tooling with Git, GNU utilities, and VS Code for repeatable engineering throughput.

Assurance

Quality, Compliance, and Security

ADN Semiconductors aligns project execution with customer quality systems, documentation expectations, and engineering governance requirements from kickoff through sign-off.

We maintain traceable review artifacts, milestone evidence, and structured communication to support auditability, decision readiness, and stakeholder confidence.

For certifications and compliance programs, we collaborate with client teams to produce required technical evidence, process records, and implementation transparency.

Our certification and assessment programs are currently in progress, including ISO 27001, ISO 9001, CMMI Level 3, and SOC 2 Type II.
