As global demand for advanced silicon grows, building a core engineering career in semiconductor design requires structured technical discipline and hands-on exposure. The ADN Semiconductors Internship Program serves as a dedicated talent incubator for aspiring engineers in Digital IC Design, RTL Design, and Functional Verification.

This guide outlines our recruitment procedures, technical syllabus, practical learning structure, and career pathways.

### **1. Selection Procedure: Step-by-Step**

Our multi-stage evaluation process focuses on core engineering fundamentals, problem-solving rigor, and practical tooling potential.

Application Submission ➔ Initial Screening ➔ Technical Written Assessment ➔ Technical Interview ➔ HR Evaluation & Offer Letter

- **Step 1: Application & Profile Screening**
  - **Target Candidates:** Final-semester undergraduate students and fresh graduates in EEE, CSE, ECE, or related engineering disciplines.

  - **Evaluation Criteria:** Academic coursework in Digital Logic Design, VLSI, and Computer Architecture, alongside relevant hardware projects and code repositories.

- **Step 2: Technical Written Assessment**
  - **Digital Logic Design:** Combinational and sequential logic design, finite state machine (FSM) implementation, timing diagrams, clock domain crossing (CDC) fundamentals, and setup/hold time constraints.

  - **Hardware Description Languages (HDLs):** Synthesizable Verilog and SystemVerilog constructs, RTL coding best practices, and hardware modeling techniques.

  - **Verification Fundamentals:** Basics of functional verification, constrained-random testing (CRV), and functional coverage metrics for robust design validation.

  - **Programming & Logic:** Algorithmic problem-solving in C/C++, basic data structures, and essential Linux command-line utilities.

  - **Recommended Preparatory Subjects:** Academic coursework or self-study in _Digital Systems Architecture_, _RTL Design Flow_, and _VLSI / Verification Methodologies_.

- **Step 3: Technical Interview**

This interview focuses on understanding your **fundamentals, projects, problem-solving approach, and potential to learn and grow**. You are **not expected to be an expert in every area**.

- Relevant academic or personal projects
- Digital logic design fundamentals
- Basic understanding of the frontend RTL design flow
- Verilog/SystemVerilog coding experience
- Basic functional verification knowledge
- Familiarity with relevant frontend tools
- Debugging and problem-solving approach
- Basic computer architecture knowledge
- Communication, teamwork, and willingness to learn

- **Step 4: HR Evaluation & Final Offer**
  - Discussion on team fitment, adaptability to collaborative engineering environments, and confirmation of full-time availability.

### **2. Technical Syllabus & Training Roadmap**

The internship follows a structured curriculum taking candidates from core design concepts to industry-standard verification flows.

Module 1: Environment & Tooling  
 ↓  
Module 2: RTL Design & Hardware Modeling  
 ↓  
Module 3: Functional Verification & Assertions  
 ↓  
Module 4: Advanced Verification & UVM

- **Module 1: Development Environment & Automation**
- **Module 2: RTL Design & Hardware Modeling (Verilog & SystemVerilog)**
- **Module 3: Functional Verification & Testbench Architecture**
- **Module 4: Industry Verification Methodology (UVM Basics)**

### **3. How to Prepare and Stand Out**

- **Solidify Fundamentals:** Focus deeply on boolean minimization, clocking fundamentals, state machines, and static timing analysis.

- **Maintain Clean Repositories:** Publish well-documented SystemVerilog/Verilog code, testbenches, and clear simulation logs on GitHub.

- **Understand the Workflow:** Gain familiarity with standard EDA simulation setups, Makefile scripts, and Linux command-line workflows.

### **4. Duration, Structure, and Logistics**

- **Duration:** 3 Months (evaluated across structured milestones).

- **Work Mode:** Full-Time, On-Site.

- **Location:** ADN Semiconductors HQ (Ambon Complex, Mohakhali, Dhaka).

- **Commitment:** Standard engineering office hours with dedicated lab access.

### **5. Learning Environment & Benefits**

- **Production-Grade Exposure:** Work directly with senior engineers on silicon-oriented design and verification workflows.

- **Modern Lab Workstations:** Access to dedicated, high-performance Linux workstations configured with enterprise EDA toolchains.

- **Structured Mentorship:** Regular one-on-one code reviews and technical feedback from practicing RTL and DV engineers.

- **Stipend:** Structured monthly compensation throughout the program.

- **Workplace Amenities:** Subsidized meals, snacks, dedicated learning resources, and an engineering-first culture.

### **6. Why Begin Your Career at ADN Semiconductors?**

- **Clear Conversion Pipeline:** High-performing interns transition directly into permanent roles such as Assistant Engineer — RTL Design and Verification.

- **Real Silicon Architecture:** Gain exposure to real-world specifications, verification plans, and workflows built for actual physical implementation.

- **Ecosystem Growth:** Position yourself at the forefront of Bangladesh’s growing semiconductor design landscape, building globally transferable technical skills.
