An evening of team bonding, meaningful discussions, and future planning at Banani Club.

On August 9, 2026, ADN Semiconductors hosted a team dinner at Banani Club for our RTL Design and Verification team. Members of the team gathered alongside our CEO and Managing Director, Mr. Faruque Amjad Khan, for an evening dedicated to connection and camaraderie.

Beyond enjoying great food and having fun together, the dinner served as an enriching opportunity for team bonding. It provided a relaxed setting for open, meaningful discussions where the team shared perspectives on our future direction, what we are aiming towards, and key plans moving forward.
