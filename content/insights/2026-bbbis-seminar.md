We are delighted to share that **ADN Semiconductors Ltd.** proudly supported the **Bay of Bengal's Business & Investment Summit 2026**, held on **January 28th** at Paragon House, Mohakhali.

During the summit, our **Senior Engineer, Foez Ahmed**, delivered a compelling presentation on the “Semiconductor Industry in Bangladesh.” He highlighted the strategic roadmap for the nation's high-tech sector, focusing on:

- The current state of Bangladesh's semiconductor sector
- Government initiatives driving semiconductor growth
- **ADN Semiconductors'** role in advancing the industry

This summit brought together industry leaders, investors, and visionaries committed to strengthening Bangladesh's position in the global tech landscape. We're excited to be part of this transformative journey!
A special thanks to Bangladesh Vision for Expo & Conference for organizing this impactful event.
Thank you to everyone who participated!
