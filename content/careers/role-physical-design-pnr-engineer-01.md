ADN Semiconductors Ltd. is hiring a Physical Design (PnR) Engineer with 2 to 3+ years of core industry experience. In this role, you will take gate-level netlists and execute block-level physical implementation through final signoff.

## Job Overview
You should be comfortable running standard EDA flows independently and handling day-to-day timing, congestion, power, and layout closure challenges. This role requires strong collaboration with RTL, STA, DFT, and verification teams to deliver signoff-ready blocks.

## Key Responsibilities
- Own block implementation including floorplanning, power grid design, placement, clock tree synthesis (CTS), and routing.
- Analyze and fix setup, hold, transition, and capacitance violations using STA reports.
- Run and close DRC, LVS, and antenna violations according to foundry signoff requirements.
- Support low-power intent implementation and checks using UPF/CPF where applicable.
- Perform basic IR drop and electromigration (EM) analysis and implement practical fixes.
- Build and maintain Tcl/Python utilities to improve runtime efficiency and reporting quality.
- Coordinate with design and project leads on risk tracking and closure milestones.

## Required Technical Skills
- BSc in EEE, ECE, CSE, or a related engineering discipline.
- 2-3+ years of hands-on digital physical design execution.
- Practical experience with at least one PnR flow: Synopsys ICC2/Fusion Compiler or Cadence Innovus.
- Practical experience with at least one STA flow: Synopsys PrimeTime or Cadence Tempus.
- Familiarity with physical verification tools such as Calibre or IC Validator.
- Comfort with Linux environments and basic Tcl or Python scripting.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in advanced nodes and multi-voltage or multi-corner signoff scenarios.
- Working knowledge of hierarchical implementation and block integration strategies.
- Exposure to ECO closure methodologies for tapeout schedules.

## What Success Looks Like
- Consistent delivery of clean implementation milestones with predictable QoR improvement.
- Signoff-ready blocks with resolved timing and physical verification issues.
- Clear communication of closure risk, mitigation plans, and handoff readiness.
