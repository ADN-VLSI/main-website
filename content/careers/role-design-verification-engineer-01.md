Join our verification team to ensure first-pass functional correctness of advanced digital IP and SoC subsystems. You will work closely with RTL designers, architecture teams, and project leads to build scalable verification strategies that balance schedule, quality, and risk.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build reusable UVM-based testbench components including agents, scoreboards, sequences, checkers, and coverage collectors.
- Develop constrained-random and directed test plans derived from architecture and micro-architecture specifications.
- Define and track functional coverage metrics, identify verification gaps, and drive closure through focused stimulus.
- Debug test failures using simulators, waveform analysis, assertions, and log triage workflows.
- Review RTL from a verification perspective and provide early feedback on testability and corner cases.
- Contribute to regression infrastructure, quality dashboards, and automation scripts for faster debug turnaround.

## Required Qualifications
- Strong hands-on experience with SystemVerilog, UVM, and assertion-based verification.
- Practical understanding of protocol verification, scoreboarding methods, and self-checking environments.
- Ability to analyze ambiguous failures and isolate root causes across testbench, RTL, and integration boundaries.
- Working knowledge of coverage-driven verification methodology and closure best practices.
- Clear communication skills for cross-functional reviews and issue tracking.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience verifying high-speed interfaces, memory subsystems, or processor-adjacent blocks.
- Familiarity with formal verification concepts and static quality checks.
- Exposure to CI-based regression flows and scripting with Python, Tcl, or shell tools.

## What Success Looks Like
- High-quality verification plans with traceability from requirements to tests and coverage.
- Consistent regression health with clear failure ownership and rapid bug turnaround.
- Verified, signoff-ready deliverables aligned with tapeout and integration milestones.
