ADN Semiconductors Ltd. is hiring an IC Design & Layout Engineer with 2 to 3+ years of industry experience. This role focuses on building high-quality custom layouts that meet circuit intent, process constraints, and integration timelines.

## Job Overview
You will transform analog, mixed-signal, and custom digital schematics into robust mask layouts. The role requires strong attention to matching, parasitics, routing discipline, and physical verification, with close collaboration across design and signoff teams.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop transistor-level and block-level custom layouts from approved schematics.
- Apply robust matching strategies such as common-centroid, interdigitation, and symmetry-aware placement.
- Optimize routing to minimize RC parasitics and protect sensitive nodes from coupling and noise.
- Execute and debug DRC, LVS, and antenna checks for full signoff compliance.
- Collaborate with circuit design engineers on post-layout simulation feedback and layout refinement.
- Maintain clean layout documentation, assumptions, and quality review artifacts.

## Required Technical Skills
- BSc in EEE, ECE, CSE, or a related engineering discipline.
- 2-3+ years of active custom IC mask layout experience.
- Practical experience with Cadence Virtuoso Layout Suite (L/XL) or Synopsys Custom Compiler.
- Practical experience with Calibre or IC Validator for physical verification.
- Strong Linux command-line comfort and understanding of semiconductor device behavior.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with deep-submicron design constraints and reliability-aware layout choices.
- Familiarity with post-layout extraction and close-loop optimization with circuit designers.
- Exposure to mixed-signal layout tradeoffs involving noise isolation, guard ring strategy, and substrate coupling.

## What Success Looks Like
- Layout deliverables that pass signoff checks with minimal rework.
- Strong layout quality that preserves circuit intent and reduces downstream integration risk.
- Predictable execution with clear status communication across project checkpoints.
