As an RTL Design Engineer, you will translate architecture intent into clean, synthesizable logic for high-performance digital products. You will play a critical role in delivering implementation quality from micro-architecture definition through signoff handoff.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop synthesizable Verilog/SystemVerilog RTL for block and subsystem features with strong modular design practices.
- Collaborate with architects to refine micro-architecture, interface timing, and corner-case behavior before coding.
- Perform lint, CDC, and static quality checks and resolve issues with a focus on signoff readiness.
- Support synthesis and timing closure by applying coding patterns that improve QoR and physical implementability.
- Partner with verification teams on test planning, bug triage, and design-for-verification improvements.
- Maintain clear design documentation for assumptions, state-machine behavior, and integration constraints.

## Required Qualifications
- Strong experience in RTL design using Verilog/SystemVerilog for complex digital logic.
- Understanding of synchronous design principles, reset/clocking strategies, and low-power-aware coding.
- Familiarity with lint, CDC/RDC, and synthesis constraints in real project environments.
- Ability to debug design issues from simulation and collaborate effectively across disciplines.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with high-speed datapath design, pipeline balancing, and performance-oriented micro-architecture.
- Exposure to formal checks, equivalence verification, and quality signoff methodologies.
- Scripting capability (Python, Tcl, shell) for automation of repetitive implementation tasks.

## What Success Looks Like
- Robust RTL that integrates cleanly and meets functionality, timing, and quality expectations.
- Reduced design rework through proactive collaboration and early risk identification.
- Predictable delivery of implementation milestones aligned with project schedules.
