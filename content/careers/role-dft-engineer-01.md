As a DFT Engineer, you will own testability planning and implementation across digital blocks and SoC-level integration. This role is central to improving manufacturing quality, reducing test cost, and enabling predictable silicon bring-up.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define DFT architecture for scan, compression, MBIST/LBIST, and boundary-scan strategies in alignment with product goals.
- Execute scan insertion and ATPG generation flows, then analyze and improve stuck-at and transition fault coverage.
- Collaborate with RTL, physical design, and timing teams to resolve DFT-related constraints and implementation tradeoffs.
- Drive DFT verification at block and top levels using simulation, pattern validation, and signoff checks.
- Support bring-up and production readiness by partnering with test and product engineering teams on pattern deployment.
- Document DFT intent, assumptions, and quality metrics for internal reviews and customer-facing milestones.

## Required Qualifications
- Proven hands-on experience with scan architecture, ATPG, and coverage analysis.
- Strong understanding of DFT rule checks, clock/reset controllability, and test-mode integration.
- Knowledge of MBIST/LBIST fundamentals and interactions with functional design behavior.
- Ability to communicate implementation risks and propose practical mitigation strategies.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with hierarchical DFT for large SoCs and multi-clock designs.
- Familiarity with low-power DFT constraints, test compression optimization, and pattern volume reduction.
- Exposure to silicon debug feedback loops and post-silicon test improvement processes.

## What Success Looks Like
- Robust DFT insertion with strong coverage and minimal impact on PPA and schedule.
- Clean DFT signoff and smooth handoff of validated test collateral to downstream teams.
- Measurable quality improvement across pre-silicon and production test milestones.
