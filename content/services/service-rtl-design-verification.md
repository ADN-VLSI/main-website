## Service Summary
We provide RTL design verification services spanning test planning, environment build-out, stimulus strategy, and closure reporting. Verification scope is mapped to product requirements and risk hotspots so effort is concentrated where failures are most costly. Our methodology emphasizes measurable coverage, reproducible regressions, and actionable debug insights.

## Key Capabilities
- Verification planning with feature-to-test traceability and risk-based prioritization
- UVM-based testbench architecture and reusable component development
- Assertion-based verification, protocol checking, and functional coverage modeling
- Regression automation, triage workflows, and root-cause debug support
- Verification signoff reviews with metrics for coverage, bug trends, and readiness

## Typical Deliverables
- Verification plan with coverage model and signoff criteria
- Reusable UVM testbench components, assertions, and checkers
- Regression infrastructure with run scripts, dashboards, and failure classification
- Verification closure report with residual-risk summary

## Target Customer Outcomes
- Lower cost by reducing late-stage bug escapes and re-spin exposure
- Improved schedule confidence through continuous regression visibility and milestone gates
- Higher quality from systematic coverage closure and requirement traceability
- Reduced project risk with quantified verification readiness before tape-out
