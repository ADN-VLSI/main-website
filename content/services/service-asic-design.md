## Service Summary
We deliver ASIC design services from specification and microarchitecture through implementation closure and handoff. Our team works with your product, system, and verification stakeholders to keep PPA targets, integration dependencies, and project milestones aligned. Engagements are structured for transparent progress tracking and predictable tape-out readiness.

## Key Capabilities
- Requirements decomposition and architecture trade-off analysis for performance, power, and area targets
- RTL development for digital subsystems with coding standards, lint clean-up, and synthesis readiness
- Clock/reset architecture definition, CDC-aware partitioning, and integration planning
- Constraint development and design handoff packages for downstream implementation
- Cross-functional design reviews with issue tracking, risk registers, and action closure

## Typical Deliverables
- Architecture and microarchitecture specifications with interface definitions
- Production-quality RTL blocks with review records and lint/synthesis reports
- Timing and design constraints aligned to implementation assumptions
- Integration checklist, risk log, and tape-out readiness documentation

## Target Customer Outcomes
- Lower program cost through reduced rework and better first-pass integration
- Improved schedule predictability with milestone-based execution and early risk burn-down
- Higher design quality through standards-driven development and structured peer reviews
- Reduced technical risk by validating architectural assumptions before late-stage implementation
